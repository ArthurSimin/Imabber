How it works

  1. DetectPage links for Discord, CurseForge, Modrinth, YouTube, Steam, GitHub, and App Store are resolved through free public APIs (japi.rest, cfwidget, Modrinth, Steam Store, iTunes, etc.). Other links are matched against known CDN patterns — thumbnail folders, size query parameters, proxy wrappers.
  2. RewriteThe URL is rewritten to that platform's maximum-quality variant (e.g. Discord ?size=4096, CurseForge without /thumbnails/…).
  3. Verify & deliverBoth versions load side by side; the larger one wins. Copy it, download it, or open it full-size.
Runs entirely in your browser · No accounts, no uploads, no tracking · Images belong to their respective owners — respect creators and site terms.
